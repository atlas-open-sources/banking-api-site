banking-api · last updated 6 November 2026

banking-api is a personal tool for its owner's own bookkeeping. It is not a service offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

  • It only reads account information (balances and transactions) for accounts its owner links. It never initiates payments.
  • Bank access runs through Enable Banking under the owner's consent, which the owner can withdraw at any time.
  • It is provided as is, with no warranty. Figures should be checked against the bank before they are relied on.
