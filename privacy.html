Privacy

banking-api · last updated 6 November 2026

banking-api is a personal, self-hosted tool that reads account data from banks through open banking (PSD2 account information) and keeps it on its owner's own computers. It has one user: the person who runs it. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else's accounts can be connected to it.

What it reads

Only for accounts the owner links themselves, with the bank's own consent screen: account names and identifiers, balances, and transactions (date, amount, currency, counterparty and description as the bank provides them). It cannot make payments or change anything at the bank.

Where the data goes

  • Requests pass through Enable Banking, a regulated account information service provider, which connects to the bank. See Enable Banking's own privacy notice for its processing.
  • The results are stored only on the owner's own devices and private cloud storage. No server, database or analytics service run by anyone else receives them.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

How long

Bank consents expire on the bank's schedule (usually 90 to 180 days) and can be withdrawn at any time in the bank's app or by the owner. Stored data is kept for the owner's bookkeeping and deleted by the owner when no longer needed.
